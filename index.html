Incentives

Start with eligibility, not assumptions

5%Corporate tax
10 or 15Years
5%Personal income tax for knowledge workers

The corporate tax rate is described for manufacturing-sector qualifying activities with capital investment, excluding land, of at least RM500 million or RM1 billion.

Sector-specific incentives, grants and facilitation support may also be available from both sides.

Source: Playbook, page 3

Tax watch-outs

Plan cross-border activity carefully

  • Define which entity performs and decides on activities.
  • Set and support fair prices for related-party transactions.
  • Monitor cross-border workdays and locations.
  • Review permanent-establishment and immigration risks.
  • Check foreign-exchange policies and transfer-pricing rules.

Source: Playbook, pages 15-20

Staff mobility

Passes for staff

Match the pass or post to the role, duration and work activity.

Key Post

A permanent post essential to safeguarding a company’s interests and investments. It is described as a high-level managerial post.

Playbook, page 28

Term Post

A temporary post for a specific period, requiring relevant qualifications and experience or skills.

Playbook, page 28

Professional Visit Pass

For temporary professional purposes such as training, consultancy or project-based work. It can last up to 12 months and is not extendable.

Playbook, pages 28-29

Investor Pass

Introduced in 2025 for eligible business travellers in Malaysia, with a stay of up to 12 months in total through a Multi-Entry Visa facility.

Playbook, page 29
